# Code of Conduct

MoL Keyboard contributors must communicate respectfully and constructively.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sexualized or personally
demeaning conduct are not accepted in project spaces.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and restrict
participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns
privately to the maintainers and include only information needed to assess the
report. Good-faith reports must not be retaliated against.
